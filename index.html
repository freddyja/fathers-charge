For the Group

Discussion Questions

Use these in your weekly meetings to go deeper.

01

What's one thing your father did well that you are intentionally passing on to your children — and one thing you are choosing to do differently?

02

Where do you feel most inadequate as a father right now? What would actually help?

03

What does it look like in your home this week to be the spiritual leader — not in a big dramatic way, but in the everyday?

04

Who is a man you consider a model father? What specifically does he do that you want to learn from?

05

If your children were asked what kind of father you are, what do you think they would say? What would you want them to say?

The Group

Nobody Hands You a Manual

Nobody hands you a manual when you find out you're going to be a father. When your turn comes, you may be carrying questions, and sometimes fear, and in some cases no where to take them. In this group we will equip fathers in all stages of fatherhood with the understanding of who God made them to be for their families.
